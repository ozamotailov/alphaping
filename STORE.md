## 6 screenshots — storyboard

Treat each like an **ad**, not a raw UI dump: a real screen + a short bold caption overlay (top
or bottom band, TON-blue `#0066B8` / white, big readable font). Capture from the Mini App in
Telegram (light or dark, be consistent). 9:16 / portrait. Caption text per slot:

| # | Screen to capture | Caption overlay (the "ad" line) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero / smart-money alert card with the **🟢 Buy on STON.fi** button | **Catch smart money the second it moves** |
| 2 | Smart-money list with PnL / win-rate scores | **Curated TON smart money — scored by real PnL** |
| 3 | New-listing alert with the safety badge + reasons | **New listings, with a built-in scam check** |
| 4 | Swap panel — TON amount, live quote, price impact | **From signal to swap in one tap** |
| 5 | Portfolio / PnL view | **See your TON holdings & PnL at a glance** |
| 6 | Watchlist / plans (Free vs Pro) or TON Connect screen | **Read-only. Your keys stay yours.** |

tokens/numbers (use a populated account) so it looks alive, not empty.
