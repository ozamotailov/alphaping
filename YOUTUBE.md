## Описание (EN, paste-ready)
TonSonar turns on-chain signals into action. It tracks TON wallets and "smart money" in real time,
pings you the instant they trade or a new jetton lists, and lets you swap in one tap — routed
through STON.fi — right inside Telegram.

In this demo:
• A smart-money buy alert lands in Telegram
• One tap opens the swap, pre-filled (no copy-paste)
• Live quote with price impact via the STON.fi SDK
• Sign in your wallet — the swap routes through STON.fi
• Portfolio & PnL update instantly

Why TonSonar:
• Real-time wallet & smart-money alerts (tonapi)
• New jetton listings on STON.fi with an anti-scam safety check
• Curated smart-money lists, scored by realized PnL & win-rate
• Portfolio / PnL
• One-tap TON → jetton swaps via the STON.fi SDK
• Read-only — we never ask for your private keys
